About

I work on growth, sales and research for early-stage companies.

I study at IIM Bangalore and at Mesa School of Business at the same time, and I work alongside founders. This year that has meant acquisition and funnel work at Kyra, sales at reform and go-to-market at Loopdesk AI.

In each of those roles my job has been the same: work out which number decides the business, then do the work that moves it. At reform that was reorder rate, at Kyra it is where drivers drop out of signup.

I have wanted to work in financial analysis since eighth grade. I keep my own portfolio, write my own reports on listed companies, and I am looking for roles in strategy consulting, investment research, banking or venture.
