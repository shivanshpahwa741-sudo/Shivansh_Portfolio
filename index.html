04

About

Somewhere between "what does the number say?" and ship it.

I have wanted to work in financial analysis since eighth grade, and most of my work since has ended up in the numbers of real businesses. I study at IIM Bangalore and Mesa School of Business, and I spend the rest of my time next to founders at Kyra, reform and Loopdesk AI.

Founders run on instinct. My job is to read the pattern objectively and either back that instinct with evidence or show why it is wrong. A problem statement is only a statement; what I enjoy is taking it to an action plan and then to scale.

Sometimes that means finding the one number that decides a business. Sometimes it means building the thing. Sometimes it means saying the idea should not exist.
